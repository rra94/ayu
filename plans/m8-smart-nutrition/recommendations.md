# Smart Food Recommendations

## What

When a micronutrient is below RDA target, suggest specific foods rich in that nutrient — ranked by bioavailability and common availability.

## How

For each nutrient below 80% RDA, show top 3-5 foods that would fill the gap. Prioritize high-bioavailability sources.

## Food Recommendations Database

### Iron (RDA: 18mg F, 8mg M)
- Beef liver (6.5mg/3oz, 25% absorbed = 1.6mg effective)
- Clams (24mg/3oz, 25% absorbed)
- Spinach (3.6mg/cup, but only 5% absorbed = 0.18mg effective — note this!)
- Lentils (3.3mg/cup, 5% but add vit C for 20%)
- Dark chocolate (3.4mg/oz)
- TIP: "Pair with vitamin C (orange, bell pepper) to boost absorption 2-6x"

### Calcium (RDA: 1000mg)
- Yogurt (300mg/cup, 30% absorbed)
- Sardines with bones (325mg/3oz)
- Fortified orange juice (350mg/cup)
- Kale (94mg/cup, 49% absorbed — better than spinach!)
- AVOID: Spinach as calcium source (only 5% bioavailable)
- TIP: "Take with vitamin D for better absorption"

### Vitamin D (RDA: 600IU)
- Salmon (570IU/3oz)
- Sardines (46IU/2 sardines)
- Egg yolk (44IU)
- Fortified milk (120IU/cup)
- TIP: "10-15 min midday sun = ~10,000IU produced by skin"

### Magnesium (RDA: 420mg M, 320mg F)
- Pumpkin seeds (156mg/oz)
- Almonds (80mg/oz)
- Spinach (78mg/cup)
- Dark chocolate (65mg/oz)
- Black beans (60mg/cup)

### Zinc (RDA: 11mg M, 8mg F)
- Oysters (74mg/3oz — highest food source)
- Beef (5mg/3oz)
- Pumpkin seeds (2.2mg/oz)
- Chickpeas (1.3mg/cup)
- TIP: "Animal sources are 2x more bioavailable than plant sources"

### Vitamin C (RDA: 90mg M, 75mg F)

- Orange (70mg)
- Kiwi (64mg)
- Broccoli (51mg/cup)
- Strawberries (49mg/cup)

### Vitamin B12 (RDA: 2.4mcg)

- Asparagus (89mcg/cup)
