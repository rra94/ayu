# M2: Home Page UX

**Depends on:** M0

## What
Redesign the Home page with a condensed layout, quick-add favorites, water tracking, daily habits checklist, gut health panel, pattern auto-logging, a 30 Plants a Week tracker, and a Blueprint Mode toggle.

## Why
The Home page is the most visited screen. It needs to surface daily progress at a glance, reduce friction for common actions, and support longevity-focused tracking without overwhelming the user.
